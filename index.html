Project 01

Bank Fraud Detection & Transaction Analysis

Unsupervised ensemble learning and behavioral risk intelligence

Photo by CardMapr.nl on Unsplash

Photo by Towfiqu barbhuiya on Unsplash

Project overview

An anomaly detection engine that identifies fraudulent activity in banking transactions. Fraud patterns change quickly, so instead of fixed rule-based alerts, the system uses an unsupervised ensemble that can catch "zero-day" anomalies: suspicious activity with no known signature.

Behavioral features are combined with several outlier-detection models, and every flag comes with an explanation of why it was raised.

Technical pipeline

  • Data engineering: numeric downcasting for speed, vectorized datetime processing, and automated encoding and scaling.
  • Exploratory analysis: distributions of amount, login attempts and balance; risk by hour, weekday and month; merchant, channel and device patterns.
  • Modeling: Isolation Forest, Local Outlier Factor, Minimum Covariance Determinant and One-Class SVM combined by majority vote to cut false alarms.
  • Governance: SHAP summaries, human-readable decision rules, and a fairness review of flag rates across customer occupations.

Explainable AI

SHAP and decision-tree rules show the "why", e.g. login attempts above 4 with an amount ratio above 2.5.

Anomalies were driven mainly by unusual login attempts, amount deviations and device activity, confirmed with t-SNE clustering.

Project 02

AdventureWorks Business Intelligence Suite

Global sales, product and customer analytics in Power BI

Project overview

This project turns raw transactional data from the AdventureWorks retail database into a multi-page, interactive Power BI suite. It gives stakeholders a 360-degree view of business health: top-level KPIs for leadership, with drill-downs into products, regional growth and customer demographics.

Data architecture & engineering

  • Star schema: one-to-many relationships between fact tables (Sales, Returns) and dimensions (Product, Customer, Territory, Calendar).
  • Power Query ETL: standardized currency, cleaned text and optimized data types across 10+ tables.
  • DAX measures: year-to-date revenue, month-over-month growth and rolling averages for return rates.

Module 4

Geospatial Performance: Regional Deep-Dive

An interactive map of the global footprint.

  • North America leads revenue, followed by Europe and Australia.
  • Order density by territory highlights untapped markets.
  • Click a continent or region to filter the entire report.

Key actionable insights

Retention opportunity

Professionals are the highest-spending segment, so marketing should focus high-end bike models on this group.

Product optimization

Accessories sell in volume at low margin. Bundling them with high-margin bikes could raise average order value.

Geographic expansion

The Pacific/Australian market has high order density for its size, making it a strong candidate for a regional warehouse.
